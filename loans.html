Mortgage Solutions That Fit Real-Life Situations

Not every borrower fits into traditional lending guidelines. Whether you're buying your first home, refinancing, investing in real estate, self-employed, purchasing with an ITIN, or qualifying through assets, I’ll help you compare available options clearly.

Conventional

  • As low as 3% down for qualified buyers
  • Strong credit may help with better pricing
  • Great for primary, second homes, and investments

FHA

  • 3.5% down payment options
  • Flexible qualification guidelines
  • Great for first-time homebuyers

VA

  • 0% down for eligible Veterans
  • No monthly mortgage insurance
  • Competitive rates and benefits

Jumbo

  • High loan amount financing
  • Primary, second home, and investment options
  • Tailored solutions for complex scenarios

Bank Statement Loans

  • Designed for self-employed borrowers
  • Qualify using 12–24 months of bank statements
  • No traditional tax-return income calculation

DSCR Investor Loans

  • Qualify based on rental property cash flow
  • No personal income verification required
  • Popular for real estate investors

ITIN Loans

  • Options for borrowers without a Social Security Number
  • Use ITIN for mortgage qualification
  • Home financing options for eligible non-U.S. citizens

Foreign National Loans

  • For non-U.S. residents buying property in the U.S.
  • Foreign income and assets may be considered
  • Options for vacation homes and investment properties

Asset Depletion Loans

  • Use retirement, brokerage, or investment assets as income
  • Helpful for retirees and high-net-worth borrowers
  • No traditional employment income required

Refinance

  • Cash-out options for projects, debt, or investments
